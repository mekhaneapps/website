How we build

  • Runs on Atlassian. Our apps are built on Forge and store data in Atlassian-hosted storage. Nothing leaves your Atlassian site to our servers, because we have none.
  • Minimal permissions. Each app asks only for the scopes it needs, and each listing explains what every scope is for.
  • Real support. Write to support@mekhaneapps.com. We answer within two business days, usually faster.
