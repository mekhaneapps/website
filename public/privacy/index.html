Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Applies to the apps published by Mekhane Apps on the Atlassian Marketplace and to this website.

1. Who we are

Mekhane Apps is the trading name of Sebastián Esperanza, an independent software developer based in Buenos Aires, Argentina (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the limited personal data described below and we, not Atlassian, are responsible for how our apps handle End User Data. Contact: support@mekhaneapps.com.

2. Our apps run on Atlassian

Our apps are built on Atlassian Forge and run entirely on Atlassian’s infrastructure. They have no servers operated by us, and they do not send data to us or to any third party. We cannot access the content of your notes or your Atlassian site.

3. What data the apps process

AppDataPurposeWhere it is stored
Rich Text Notes for JiraThe content of each note (formatted text you write), the Atlassian account ID of the person who last saved it, and the time of the last save.Showing the note on the dashboard and attributing the last change.Atlassian Forge hosted storage for your site, within Atlassian’s data residency arrangements.

The apps request only the Atlassian permissions (“scopes”) they need. Rich Text Notes requests storage:app only; it cannot read issues, projects, users or any other data in your site.

4. Data retention and deletion

Note content stays in Forge storage while the app is installed. Deleting a gadget does not immediately delete its stored note; uninstalling the app makes all stored data subject to Atlassian’s Forge storage retention and deletion rules. You can ask us to help remove specific data at any time by writing to the address above; because we do not hold the data, such requests are carried out through the app itself or by your Atlassian administrator.

5. Data we collect from you directly

If you email us, we keep your message and address to answer you and to keep a record of support history. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. This website

This website is static. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record your IP address under Google’s privacy terms.

7. Atlassian

Atlassian processes data as described in the Atlassian Privacy Policy and operates the Forge platform on which our apps run. Marketplace purchases are handled by Atlassian; we receive aggregated licensing information (site name, plan, user tier) but not payment details.

8. Security

We rely on Atlassian’s platform security for data at rest and in transit. Our apps sanitize all content before displaying it and store no credentials. Security concerns: support@mekhaneapps.com.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Write to us and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your supervisory authority. Argentine users may contact the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública.

10. Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify administrators of installed apps through the Marketplace.
