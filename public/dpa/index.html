Data Processing Agreement

Effective 20 October 2026 · Version 1.0 · Applies to customers of Mekhane Apps on the Atlassian Marketplace.

1. Parties and scope

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) is between the customer that installs an app published by Mekhane Apps on the Atlassian Marketplace (“Customer”, the controller) and Sebastián Esperanza, trading as Mekhane Apps, Buenos Aires, Argentina (“Mekhane Apps”, the processor). It forms part of the terms under which the Customer uses the app (the Atlassian Marketplace standard end user agreement and the provider-specific terms) and applies whenever an app processes Personal Data on the Customer’s behalf. It is accepted by installing or continuing to use the app; no signature is required. A countersigned copy is available on request.

Terms such as “Personal Data”, “processing”, “controller”, “processor”, “sub-processor” and “Personal Data Breach” have the meanings given in the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (“GDPR”). This DPA also applies, with the necessary changes, under the UK GDPR, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and Argentina’s Law 25,326.

3. How processing happens

All Mekhane Apps are built on Atlassian Forge and run entirely on Atlassian’s infrastructure. Data is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage or inside the Customer’s own Confluence pages, within the data residency location of the Customer’s Atlassian site. The apps send no data to servers operated by Mekhane Apps (we operate none) or to any other third party. Mekhane Apps has no technical means to read the Customer’s stored data in the normal course of business.

4. Processor obligations

  • Instructions. Mekhane Apps processes Personal Data only on the Customer’s documented instructions. The app’s functionality, its documentation and this DPA are those instructions. If Mekhane Apps believes an instruction infringes data protection law, it will tell the Customer.
  • Confidentiality. Mekhane Apps is a sole proprietorship. Anyone who may in future help provide the apps will be bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • No other use. Personal Data is not sold, used for advertising, used to train models, or combined with other data.

5. Security

Mekhane Apps implements the measures described in this section, appropriate to the limited and pseudonymous nature of the data:

  • Data minimisation: only account IDs are stored, never names, emails or other profile attributes.
  • Storage only in Atlassian Forge hosted storage, protected by Atlassian’s platform security (encryption in transit and at rest, access isolation between apps and sites).
  • No external network access: the apps declare no egress and qualify for Atlassian’s “Runs on Atlassian” program.
  • Minimum permission scopes, explained in each app’s Marketplace listing.
  • Content is sanitised before it is displayed; application logs contain no Personal Data.
  • Developer accounts are protected with two-step verification.

7. Assistance to the Customer

  • Data subject requests. Most requests can be handled directly in the app (reminders can be viewed, edited and deleted by their creators). For anything else, Mekhane Apps will assist within 10 business days of a request to support@mekhaneapps.com.
  • Impact assessments and consultations. Mekhane Apps will provide the information it reasonably has to support the Customer’s data protection impact assessments.

8. Personal Data Breaches

Mekhane Apps will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting the Customer’s data. The notice will describe the breach, the data and people likely affected, likely consequences and the measures taken. Notices go to the Customer’s Atlassian site administrators or the contact the Customer provides.

9. Deletion and return

When the app is uninstalled, its Forge storage is deleted by Atlassian under its Forge data retention rules. Deleting an issue deletes its reminders immediately. At the Customer’s request before uninstalling, Mekhane Apps will help export the Customer’s reminder data.

10. Audits

Mekhane Apps will make available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including written answers to reasonable security questionnaires within 30 days. Because the apps run on Atlassian’s infrastructure, the Customer agrees that Atlassian’s own certifications and audit reports cover the hosting environment.

11. International transfers

Mekhane Apps is established in Argentina, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection (Decision 2003/490/EC). Personal Data stays in Atlassian’s infrastructure in the Customer’s data residency location; Mekhane Apps does not transfer it elsewhere. Any transfer that becomes necessary will use a valid mechanism under applicable law, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Liability and precedence

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the terms governing the Customer’s use of the app. If this DPA conflicts with those terms on the processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails.

13. Contact

Data protection questions and requests: support@mekhaneapps.com (subject “Data protection”). See also the privacy policy.
